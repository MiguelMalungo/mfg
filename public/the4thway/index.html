THE FOURTH WAY

Everyone here walks asleep, each drawn by a thread toward the moon.
First read the standing stones: raise your lantern at each one to take its knowledge (a diamond).
Then find the sleeper who carries a spark of gold, raise your light to wake them, and follow.

Walking costs you presence (the ring at the bottom). Stand still and hold Space to remember yourself and refill it; while you do, the gold spark shows you where to go.
If the ring empties, you sleepwalk for a few seconds. Nothing is lost, only time.
