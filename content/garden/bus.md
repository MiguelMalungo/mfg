Before the truck there was a bus. I converted it and lived in it for a
stretch. It's where the taste for this came from — the discovery that a
small, well-made space is more than enough, and that the view changing
is a kind of furniture.

The bus is done. The next chapter is [[life-on-the-road|the truck]].
